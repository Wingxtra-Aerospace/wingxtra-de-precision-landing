Camera-to-body rotation

OpenCV camera axes → aircraft forward, right, down. The default is for a downward camera with image top toward the nose. Enter a proper 3×3 rotation matrix.

Camera position offsets belong in ArduPilot PLND_CAM_POS_X/Y/Z. Do not add them to this rotation. Configure fixed focus and disable digital stabilization or variable cropping at the camera.

Connect through the onboard router

The flight-controller serial link remains owned by BlueOS or DroneEngage.

In BlueOS MAVLink Endpoints, add a UDP client destination 127.0.0.1:14771 (or your selected address and port). Incoming autopilot heartbeats establish the return path.

DroneEngage transport settings

Requires a separate raw MAVLink telemetry feed to the listen endpoint above, plus a verified local DataBus deployment. Only one target publisher may be active.

Measurement acceptance limits

These are installation limits, not guaranteed performance. Validate them against recorded camera latency, expected relative speed and flight tests.
